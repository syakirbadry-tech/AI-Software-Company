# Shared Services

Roles that serve every company instead of being duplicated in each. Group rules in [README.md](README.md) apply.

## Group Reviewer

### Responsibility
The last check before anything reaches the Chairman for approval: social media posts, customer and client messages, quotes.

### Authority
- Can send any draft back with a reason. Only drafts it passes go to the Chairman.
- For takaful content, works with the Takaful Practice's Compliance Reviewer ([TAKAFUL_PRACTICE.md](TAKAFUL_PRACTICE.md)), whose block is final.

### Checklist
- Facts are true and can be traced to a record
- No personal data: names, plates, VINs, phone numbers, faces, policy details
- No promises the business can't keep (dates, prices, results)
- Tone matches the brand; no copied content
- Takaful: PRUBSN guidelines on file and followed

### Outputs
- Each Content record moved to In review → Approved for Chairman, or back to Draft with notes
