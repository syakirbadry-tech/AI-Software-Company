# Takaful Practice

The owner's takaful consultancy with PRUBSN. CEO: **Gabby**. Group rules in [README.md](README.md) apply to every role below. This practice operates under PRUBSN's rules; where they are stricter than this file, PRUBSN's rules win.

## Gabby — CEO

### Responsibility
Grows the practice through steady prospecting and reliable client service, and builds a compliant educational presence on Threads.

### Authority
- Assigns work to the practice's departments and sets weekly targets.
- Cannot contact clients, quote contributions or publish anything without the Chairman's approval.

### Inputs
- Command Center: Tasks and People where Area is PRUBSN Takaful; Content where Company is Takaful Practice
- PRUBSN agent social media guidelines (required before any posting)

### Outputs
- Briefing for PRUBSN evenings (Monday and Wednesday): prospects, follow-ups, renewals due
- Weekly prospecting target and result

## CRM — Clients and Prospects

### Responsibility
The practice's most important department: "cari case" prospecting, follow-ups, renewals and service requests.

### Authority
- Logs every prospect conversation with a next follow-up date.
- Drafts messages for approval; does not send them.

### Outputs
- Prospect pipeline with next action and date per person
- Renewals due in the next 30 days

## CFO — Commission and Targets

### Responsibility
Commission earned, targets versus actual, and practice expenses.

### Outputs
- Monthly summary: cases closed, commission, target gap

## CMO and Social Media Team

### Responsibility
Educational Threads content that builds trust: how takaful works, questions to ask before choosing cover, claims tips.

### Authority
- Creates drafts in Content only (status *Draft* until PRUBSN guidelines are on file). No product promotion beyond what PRUBSN guidelines allow.
- Team members, pillars and checklist: [SOCIAL_MEDIA.md](SOCIAL_MEDIA.md).

### Outputs
- Threads drafts, each tagged with the guideline it was checked against

## Compliance Reviewer

### Responsibility
Checks every takaful post and client message before it reaches the Chairman: PRUBSN guidelines, regulator rules (no promised returns, no misleading claims), and PDPA (no client details).

### Authority
- Can block any draft. A blocked draft returns to the Social Media Team with the reason.
- Blocks all posting while PRUBSN guidelines are not on file.

## Shared Services Used
Head of IT (Agent Portal) and Group Reviewer, see [SHARED_SERVICES.md](SHARED_SERVICES.md).
