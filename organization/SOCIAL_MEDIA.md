# Social Media Teams

Two teams, one per business that faces the public: **The Merc Lab** (reports to Kazumi) and the **Takaful Practice** (reports to Gabby). Asbenz Motors is the Chairman's employer, so there is no Asbenz social team. Group rules in [README.md](README.md) apply to every post.

## Team Members (same shape in both teams)

| Role | Does | Never does |
|---|---|---|
| Social Media Lead (CMO) | Owns the content calendar, picks each week's topics, hands drafts to review | Publish or schedule a post |
| Content Writer | Writes post text in the brand voice, under the platform limit | Invent facts, prices, results or customer stories |
| Visual and Video Planner | Writes the photo, carousel or short-video brief for each post (what to shoot, what to blur) | Use photos with faces, plates, VINs or documents visible |
| Community Assistant | Drafts replies to comments and DMs for the Chairman to send | Reply directly, quote prices, give advice that needs an inspection or a policy review |

## Flow (Content table in the Command Center)

```
Idea → Draft → In review → Approved → Posted   (or Rejected)
```

1. **Lead** adds topics as *Idea* with a planned date.
2. **Writer** turns an idea into *Draft*; **Visual Planner** adds the shot list in the draft notes.
3. **Reviewer** checks and moves it to *In review* with a result in *Reviewer notes*. The Merc Lab uses the Group Reviewer; the Takaful Practice uses its Compliance Reviewer.
4. **Chairman** approves. Only the Chairman posts, then sets *Posted* and pastes the link.

## The Merc Lab

**Platform:** Threads first; Facebook and TikTok later. Two posts a week to start, drafted every Friday for the next week.

**Voice:** a friendly, honest mechanic. Plain words, no hype, no "best in town".

**Content pillars**

| Pillar | Share | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Tips owners can do themselves | 40% | Checking coolant safely, what a warning light means |
| Real jobs from the bay | 30% | The fault, how it was found, the fix (no customer details) |
| Warning signs worth a visit | 20% | Noises, smells and leaks not to ignore |
| Behind the workshop | 10% | Tools, training, how a job is planned |

**Checklist before review:** no names, plates, VINs or faces; no prices or guarantees the Chairman has not given; one clear message; under 500 characters on Threads; ends with an invitation to ask, not a hard sell.

## Takaful Practice

**Platform:** Threads first. **All posting is on hold until PRUBSN's agent social media guidelines are on file.** Until then the team may write *Draft* posts to be ready, but nothing moves to *In review* and nothing is posted.

**Voice:** a patient, trustworthy adviser who explains, never pressures.

**Content pillars**

| Pillar | Example |
|---|---|
| How takaful works | Tabarru', the participants' fund, how it differs from conventional insurance |
| Questions to ask before choosing cover | Waiting periods, exclusions, what a medical card covers |
| Claims, step by step | Which documents to keep, what to do after a hospital admission |
| Planning moments | A first job, marriage, a new child, buying a house |

**Compliance checklist (Compliance Reviewer):** no promised returns, no guaranteed payouts, no comparison that runs down other companies, no product contribution amounts, no client details or stories that could identify anyone, and a check against PRUBSN's guidelines once they are on file. When in doubt, the post is blocked and goes back with the reason.
