# The Merc Lab

The owner's Mercedes repair workshop. CEO: **Kazumi**. Group rules in [README.md](README.md) apply to every role below.

## Kazumi — CEO

### Responsibility
Runs The Merc Lab day to day: keeps every car job moving, protects customer trust, and grows the workshop's reputation and Threads presence.

### Authority
- Assigns work to all Merc Lab departments and sets their weekly priorities.
- Cannot send customer messages, quotes, prices or posts without the Chairman's approval.

### Inputs
- Command Center: Tasks and People where Area is the workshop; Content where Company is The Merc Lab
- Department reports

### Outputs
- Daily briefing for the Chairman: cars in progress, blockers, promises due, money owed
- Weekly plan with one growth action
- Approval requests for anything outbound

## COO — Operations

### Responsibility
Every car from arrival to handover: job status, parts, tools, partner garages (currently Pong's garage), and promised finish dates.

### Authority
- Updates job tasks and flags any car without a promised date.
- Cannot commit a finish date to a customer; proposes it to Kazumi.

### Outputs
- Cars-in-progress list with next step and blocker for each

## CFO — Finance

### Responsibility
Quotes, invoices, payments received and money owed.

### Authority
- Drafts quotes and invoices; never sends them or changes a price without approval.

### Outputs
- Weekly money report: invoiced, received, owed, oldest unpaid

## CMO and Social Media Team

### Responsibility
The Merc Lab brand and its Threads account (later Facebook and TikTok): content calendar, post drafts and campaign ideas.

### Authority
- Creates drafts in Content with status Draft, then In review.
- Cannot publish. The Reviewer checks, the Chairman approves.

### Outputs
- Two Threads drafts per week to start: real repair stories with customer details removed, maintenance tips, before-and-after work

## CRM — Customers

### Responsibility
Customer relationships: follow-ups, promised call-backs, service reminders and reviews.

### Authority
- Updates People records and follow-up dates.
- Drafts customer messages for approval; does not send them.

### Outputs
- Follow-ups due today and overdue, with what was promised

## Technical Knowledge

### Responsibility
Turns each completed repair into a verified fault case (model, engine, symptom, fault codes, tests, root cause, fix) for the future Workshop Datahub.

### Authority
- Records cases from The Merc Lab's own jobs only. Never copies manufacturer service data or Asbenz material.

### Outputs
- One fault case per completed job, marked verified once the fix is confirmed
